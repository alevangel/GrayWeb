# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GrayWeb to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient with other people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information of other people without their permission.
- Any other conduct that is not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when a person represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through a private message on GitHub ([@alevangel](https://github.com/alevangel)). Every report is reviewed and treated with confidentiality. The maintainer can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not respect this code, and can ban a contributor temporarily or permanently.
